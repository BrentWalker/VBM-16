WELCOME

Your phone is the sampler

  • Film anything: a drum pad, a guitar, a voice, the truck.
  • VBM-16 chops it into pads. Hit a pad and you hear the sound and see that moment of video.
  • Sequence the pads into a beat and export it as a music video that loops clean.

STEP 1

Know your buttons

  • ▶ plays and stops. Tap the red dot to record pad hits live. While it plays, hold the red dot and hold a pad to erase that pad as the playhead passes.
  • - 92 + is the tempo. The metronome clicks each beat (it never shows up in exports).
  • The pizza opens the slicer. The keyboard turns pads into notes, and its chord row (MAJ, MIN, 7, m7, SUS, KEY) makes one key play a whole chord. The eye switches the video between MIX (every sound playing gets its own piece of the screen), FOCUS (big round eye: follows your selected track), 1 (latest hit) and 4 (one per track).

STEP 2

Add a video

  • Tap the camera at the top to film or pick a video. (In the slicer, + VIDEO does the same.)
  • Choose AUTO CHOP to fill the next empty bank with 16 chops, or JUST UPLOAD to chop it yourself.
  • Pick what's in the video. Drums get tight hits, vox gets phrases, chaos gets random chops.
  • VBM-16 reads the key of the video and shows it on its tab, like V1 Cm. Pads with a clear pitch show their note, and KEYS mode labels them with real note names.
  • On iPhone it plays the clip silently once to grab the sound, so give it a few seconds.

STEP 3

Chop it yourself

  • Drag either edge on the waveform. Edges can cross, and whichever is on the left becomes the start.
  • Hold inside the selection until it lights up, then drag to slide the whole slice.
  • Pinch to zoom. Tap the wave to hear a spot. Zoomed in and an edge is off screen? Slide in from the left side to pull the start to you, from the right side to pull the end.
  • Press the big ▶ with LOOP on and nudge with [ and ] until it's clean. ½, ×2 and the note buttons set the length.

STEP 4

Line up the picture

  • Phone cameras often show the picture a little late compared with the sound.
  • VIDEO SLIP moves the picture earlier or later, up to 1 second, while the sound stays put.
  • The red bar shows where the video now comes from. Loop it and tap ◀ until your finger lands right on the hit.
  • Slip stays set for that video, so every chop from it is already lined up.

STEP 5

Send chops to pads

  • Under SEND TO PAD, tap a pad and the slice drops in. The next pad lights up, so you can chop, tap, chop, tap.
  • You get 8 banks of 16, so 128 chops. Tap a mini block to pick a bank. AUTO CHOP fills the next empty bank and never overwrites one.
  • Hold a pad and drag it onto another pad, or onto a tiny square in the bank strip, then pick SWAP or REPLACE.
  • Hold a bank until it lifts, then drag it to reorder. Your patterns follow the sounds. To clear, hold the red button under the pads and tap a bank or pad.

STEP 6

Build a beat

  • Pick a track (A to D), tap a pad, then tap steps to place it. Pick another pad and tap the same step to stack up to 4 sounds. Tap a step with one of its pads selected to take that one out, or double tap to clear the whole step. Hold a page box and tap steps off to make a bar shorter than 16. M mutes a track.
  • Pads ring over each other. Put pads in the same MUTE GRP to make them cut each other off, like open and closed hats. Hold a bank and tap a group to set all 16 at once.
  • Arm the red dot and play pads while it runs to record live. Hits snap to the grid.
  • LNGTH makes a pattern 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars. A step like 2:4 means bank 2, pad 4.

STEP 7

Shape it

  • FX: hold to punch in, double tap to lock it on (it turns red), tap again to release. Every effect changes both the sound and the picture.
  • Track FX: the row under FX has a slot for each track, A to D. Hold a slot to pick up to 2 effects for that track: GAIN, DRV, LCUT, HCUT, LBST, HBST, VERB or DLY, each with an amount slider. Tap it to turn them on and off.
  • Pad edit: ONE SHOT plays the whole chop, GATE stops when you let go, LOOP repeats while held.
  • FADE and DECAY clean up and shorten. FREEZE holds one frame. EDIT SLICE reopens the pad in the slicer.
  • Drag the bar under the video to resize it. The VIDEO FX panel has FIT or FILL, how MIX shares the screen (STACK, GRID, PIP, COLLAGE, DIAGONAL), picture FX (ZOOM, SHK, VHS, DUO), WAVE and your producer TAG. It all shows in exports.

STEP 8

Make it a song

  • You have 16 patterns. Tap one while playing and it starts when the current one ends.
  • Turn on CHAIN and tap patterns in order to build the song, like verse, verse, hook, bridge.
  • COPY TO NEXT duplicates a pattern so you can change just the ending.
  • Messed up? ↶ undoes it and ↷ brings it back.

STEP 9

Save and share

  • Tap the floppy disk at the top. SAVE SESSION keeps everything in one file. On iPhone, choose Save to Files. OPEN brings it back.
  • INIT in the same window clears just the patterns, just the pads, or everything, including all FX. Undo brings it back.
  • Work also autosaves on your phone. Full original videos aren't saved, so add a video again if you want to re-chop it.
  • EXPORT records your pattern or chain on the beat, looped up to 16 times, in 9:16 for TikTok and Reels or 1:1 and 16:9. Keep VBM-16 on screen until the clip appears.
  • Pinch the video to zoom in. Tap any panel title to shrink it. Hold a shrunk panel's title to drag it into the order you like. VIDEO stays on top.
