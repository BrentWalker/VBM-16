Quick swipe tutorial · VBM-16 v1.1

Session: one .zip with every pad's sound and video, all patterns, pad settings, BPM and swing. With INCLUDE VIDEOS on, it also keeps the full videos so EDIT SLICE and VIDEO SLIP still work when you open it again. Turn it off for a much smaller file.

Samples: a .zip of every pad sound as WAV files for any DAW.

Load samples: pick WAV or MP3 files (or a .zip of them). They fill pads starting from the selected pad.

Your work also autosaves in this browser and comes back when you reopen the page. The full original videos are not saved, so add a video again if you want to re-slice or slip pads from it.
