VBM-16

Getting started · Version 1 · BeatSmith

VBM-16 turns any video into a drum kit. Chop your clips onto pads, sequence a beat, and every hit plays its own piece of video. Export it as a music video that loops clean.

Put it on your iPhone

1
Open the app in Safari

brentwalker.github.io/VBM-16

2
Add to Home Screen

Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

3
Open it from the icon

It runs full screen, works without signal after the first open, and brings your last pads and patterns back.

Make your first beat

1
Add a video

Tap the camera at the top. Film something or pick a clip from Photos or Files.

2
Pick what's in it

Choose AUTO CHOP and a type like DRUMS. VBM-16 listens to the clip, reads its key and fills the next empty bank with 16 chops.

3
Pick a track and a pad

Tap track A, then tap a pad. You hear the hit and see its video.

4
Place steps

Tap steps in the sequencer to drop that pad on the beat. Tap a step again to clear it.

5
Play it

Tap ▶. Turn on the metronome if you want a click. Set the tempo with - and +.

6
Build it up

Put other sounds on tracks B, C and D. Pads from any bank can go on any track.

7
Shape it

Hold an effect in the FX row, or hold a track slot under it to give a whole track reverb, drive or delay.

8
Save and export

Tap the floppy disk to save your session. Tap EXPORT to record your beat as a video.

The screen, top to bottom

Top bar

Floppy disk opens the save window, with this guide at the top. Camera adds a video. ↶ undo and ↷ redo cover your last 60 changes. EXPORT opens the export window.

Video

Shows your hits as they play. Pinch to zoom in, drag to look around, double tap to zoom back out. Drag the bar under it to resize, double tap the bar for small, medium, large and tall.

Transport

▶ play, red dot live record, tempo, metronome, pizza opens the slicer, keyboard turns on KEYS, eye picks the video view.

VIDEO FX

FIT or FILL, MIX layout, WAVE, TAG, and the picture effects ZOOM, SHK, VHS and DUO.

FX and track FX

The top row punches effects into everything. The row under it holds 2 effects per track.

Sequencer

Tracks A to D with mute, LEN, bar pages, FOLLOW, the 16 steps and the nudge row.

Banks

The 8 bank blocks, the 16 pads and the red clear button.

Pad edit, Patterns, Groove

Everything about the selected pad, the 16 patterns and the chain, BPM and swing.

Tap any panel title to minimize it. Hold a minimized title to drag it into a new order. VIDEO and the transport always stay on top.

Video types

DRUMSTight hits up to 1 s, sorted kick, snare, hat
BASSNote chops up to 2 s
GUITARNote chops up to 2 s
KEYSNote chops up to 2 s
VOXPhrase chops up to 3 s with soft fades
SAMPLESRecord and loop chops up to 2 s
FXRisers, hits and sweeps up to 3 s with long fade outs
CHAOSRandom spots and lengths, some flipped backwards

AUTO CHOP fills the next empty bank and never overwrites one. JUST UPLOAD opens the slicer so you chop it yourself.

Scale reader: every video gets its key read, like V1 Cm on its tab. Pads with a clear pitch also show their note.

Chop it yourself (the slicer)

1
Open the slicer

Tap the pizza. Each video is a tab across the top. + VIDEO adds another.

2
Set the slice

Drag either edge on the waveform. Edges can pass each other, whichever is on the left is the start. Tap the wave to hear a spot, pinch to zoom.

3
Slide it

Hold inside the selection until it lights up, then drag to move the whole slice. Zoomed in with an edge off screen? Slide in from the left side to pull the start, from the right side to pull the end.

4
Check the loop

Press the big ▶ with LOOP on. Nudge with [ and ]. The middle button sets 1 ms, 10 ms or 1 frame. ½, ×2 and the note buttons set the length to the beat.

5
Line up the picture

VIDEO SLIP moves the picture up to 1 second earlier or later while the sound stays put. The red bar shows where the video now comes from.

6
Send it

Tap a pad under SEND TO PAD. The next pad lights up as NEXT, so you can chop, tap, chop, tap. The bank blocks above pick which bank you fill.

Banks and pads

8 banks of 16

128 chops in all. Tap a bank block to put it on the pads. Hold a block until it lifts, then drag it to reorder. Your patterns follow the sounds.

Clear

Hold the red button at the bottom of BANKS and tap a bank block or a pad. Double tap the red button to keep clear mode on. Undo brings it back.

Play modes

ONE SHOT plays the whole chop. GATE stops when you let go, one step in the sequencer. LOOP repeats while held, and in the sequencer until that pad plays again.

Mute groups

Pads ring over each other. Put pads in the same MUTE GRP 1 to 4 to make them cut each other off, like an open and closed hat.

Shape a pad

Pitch, level, reverse, fade in, fade out, decay, freeze frame and video slip. EDIT SLICE reopens it in the slicer while its video is loaded.

Sequencer and patterns

Tracks

A to D. Pads play on the selected track. M mutes a track. A step shows bank:pad, like 2:4, when the sound is from another bank.

Longer patterns

LEN makes a pattern 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars. New bars start as copies. The numbers pick which bar you edit, FOLLOW makes the view follow the playhead.

Live record

Arm the red dot and play pads while it runs. Hits snap to the nearest step.

Nudge

Hold a step until it gets a dashed outline, then push it early or late with ◀ and ▶. ALL nudges the whole track for a laid back or pushed feel.

Stacks

A step can hold up to 4 sounds. Pick another pad and tap the same step to add it. The step shows a small number for how many it holds. Tap a step with one of its pads selected to take just that one out. Nudge moves the whole stack together.

KEYS and chords

The 16 pads become 16 notes of the selected pad, labeled with note names when it has a clear pitch. In KEYS, the chord row picks what one key plays: OFF single notes, MAJ, MIN, 7, m7, SUS, or KEY, which builds each chord from the key the scale reader found, so every chord fits the song. Tap a step to place the whole chord, and live record catches it too.

Patterns and chain

16 patterns. A new pattern starts when the current one ends. Turn on CHAIN and tap patterns in order to build a song. COPY TO NEXT duplicates one.

FX

LOW · HIGHFilters on everything
1/2Slows the beat 25%, pitch stays
ECHOEcho in time with the beat
ST8 · ST16Stutter in 8th or 16th notes
REVHits play backwards
OCT↓An octave down, slowed like tape

Hold to punch in, double tap to lock it on, tap again to release. Most of them change the picture too: LOW blurs, HIGH inverts, ECHO leaves trails, REV tints, OCT↓ slows the video.

Track FX (2 per track)

GAINLevel, -12 to +12 dB
DRVDrive, light grit to heavy
LCUT · HCUTLow cut and high cut
LBST · HBSTLow boost and high boost, up to +12 dB
VERBReverb
DLYDelay in time with the beat

Hold a track slot to pick up to 2 and set each one's amount. Tap the slot to turn them on and off.

Video views and VIDEO FX

MIXEvery sound playing gets its own piece of the screen
FOCUSBig round eye: follows your selected track
1Only the latest hit
4One quarter per track
MIX layouts

STACK, GRID, PIP (new hits in corner windows), COLLAGE (hits land at random spots) and DIAGONAL.

Picture FX

ZOOM punches in on hits, SHK shakes, VHS adds scanlines and wobble, DUO tints each track its own color.

WAVE and TAG

WAVE draws the playing sound's waveform over the video. TAG puts your producer tag on every frame, type it and pick a corner.

Everything you see is in your exports. Zooming the player is only for you and doesn't change exports.

Save and export

Floppy disk

SAVE SESSION keeps every pad, pattern and setting in one .zip. OPEN brings it back. SAVE SAMPLES gives you WAV files for any DAW. LOAD SAMPLES puts your own audio on pads.

INIT

In the floppy disk window: clear just the patterns, just the pads, or everything. Undo brings pads and patterns back.

Export

Pick the length (pattern, 1 to 8 bars or the whole chain), how many times it loops (1× to 16×) and the shape (9:16, 1:1, 16:9 or your first video), then RECORD. It starts on the downbeat and ends right on the bar.

In the home screen app, tap the save button when the file is ready, then pick Save to Files or Save Video. Work also autosaves on your phone. Full original videos aren't saved, so add a video again to re-chop it.

Tips for better beats

Shoot at 30 fps

Steady light, phone still, the sound close to the mic. Clean hits chop better.

Chords that fit

Chop one clean note, turn on KEYS and pick KEY in the chord row. Every key plays a chord that belongs to your video's key.

Match keys

Check the key on each video's tab before mixing samples, so your chops sit together.

Hats in a mute group

Put the open and closed hat in the same group so the closed one chokes the open one.

Fatten the low end

LBST on the 808 track and LCUT on the sample track keeps the bass clear.

Fix late video once

Phone cameras often show the picture a few frames late. Set VIDEO SLIP in the slicer and every chop from that video comes out lined up.

Brand every export

Turn on TAG and every video you post carries your name.

Fixes

No sound after recording with the camera

Recording turns on the iPhone mic, which can interrupt VBM-16's sound. If a clip comes in silent, tap TRY AGAIN. Still silent: make sure the ring switch isn't on silent, or record in the Camera app and add the clip with the camera icon.

"Listening to the audio" takes a while

On iPhone, VBM-16 plays the clip silently once to grab the sound, about as long as the clip, up to the first 60 seconds.

The finger lands late on the hit

Use VIDEO SLIP in the slicer and tap ◀ one frame at a time until it lines up.

"All 8 banks are full"

Auto chop never overwrites. Clear a bank with the red button in BANKS, then add the video again.

A click at the start or end of a chop

Raise FADE IN or FADE OUT on that pad a few ms.

The key reads wrong

Busy drums and noisy clips are hard to read. Trust your ears and use the second choice it shows when it isn't sure.

The update isn't showing

Close the home screen app fully and open it again while you have signal.
