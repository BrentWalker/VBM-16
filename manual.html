VBM-16

Getting started · Version 1.0 · BeatSmith

VBM-16 turns any video into a drum kit. Chop your clips onto pads, sequence a beat, and every hit plays its own piece of video. Export it as a music video that loops clean.

Put it on your iPhone

1
Open the app in Safari

brentwalker.github.io/VBM-16

2
Add to Home Screen

Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

3
Open it from the icon

It runs full screen, works without signal after the first open, and brings your last pads and patterns back.

Make your first beat

1
Add a video

Tap the camera at the top. Film something or pick a clip from Photos or Files.

2
Pick what's in it

Choose AUTO CHOP and a type like DRUMS. VBM-16 listens to the clip, reads its key and fills the next empty bank with 16 chops.

3
Pick a track and a pad

Tap track A, then tap a pad. You hear the hit and see its video.

4
Place steps

Tap steps in the sequencer to drop that pad on the beat. Tap a step again to clear it.

5
Play it

Tap ▶. Turn on the metronome if you want a click. Set the tempo with - and +.

6
Build it up

Put other sounds on tracks B, C and D. Pads from any bank can go on any track.

7
Shape it

Hold an effect in the AUDIO FX row, or hold a track slot under it to give a whole track reverb, drive or delay.

8
Save and export

Tap the floppy disk to save your session. Tap EXPORT to record your beat as a video.

The screen, top to bottom

iPad sideways

On an iPad (or any wide screen) held sideways, VBM-16 splits into two columns that scroll on their own. Left: the top bar, video, transport, VIDEO FX and FX. Right: sequencer, banks and the rest. Hold the left part of any panel title and drag to move it within a column or across to the other one. Hold the VIDEO title and drag left or right to swap the video to the other side; the top bar and transport go with it and stay on top. The WAVEFORM panel runs across the bottom. Turn it upright and it goes back to one column.

Top bar

Floppy disk opens the save window, with this guide at the top. Camera adds a video. ↶ undo and ↷ redo cover your last 60 changes. EXPORT opens the export window.

Video

Shows your hits as they play. Pinch to zoom in, drag to look around, double tap to zoom back out. Drag the bar under it to resize, double tap the bar for small, medium, large and tall.

Transport

▶ play, red dot live record, tempo, metronome, keyboard turns on KEYS, eye picks the video view, and the red speaker with an X stops every sound at once (the beat, held pads, loops and echo or reverb tails).

VIDEO FX

FIT or FILL, MIX layout, WAVE, TAG, and the picture effects ZOOM, SHK, VHS and DUO.

AUDIO FX and track FX

The top row punches effects into everything. The row under it holds 2 effects per track.

STEP SEQUENCER

Tracks A to D with mute, LNGTH, bar pages, FOLLOW, the 16 steps and the nudge row.

PAD BANKS

The 8 bank blocks, the 16 pads and the red clear button.

PAD EDIT, PATTERNS, GROOVE

Everything about the selected pad, the 16 patterns and the chain, BPM and swing.

Tap anywhere on a panel title to fold or open it. To move a panel, hold the left part of its title (about as wide as the words STEP SEQUENCER) until it lifts, then drag it into a new order. This works open or folded. VIDEO and the transport always stay on top.

Video types

DRUMSTight hits up to 1 s, sorted kick, snare, hat
BASSNote chops up to 2 s
GUITARNote chops up to 2 s
KEYSNote chops up to 2 s
VOXPhrase chops up to 3 s with soft fades
SAMPLESRecord and loop chops up to 2 s
TRANSIENTFinds every hit in the video, divides them by 16 and keeps every Nth one, so the chops spread across the whole clip. Each chop runs to the next.
EQUALCuts the whole video into 16 equal parts

AUTO CHOP fills the next empty bank and never overwrites one. JUST UPLOAD puts it in the WAVEFORM panel so you chop it yourself.

Scale reader: every video gets its key read, like V1 Cm on its tab. Pads with a clear pitch also show their note.

The WAVEFORM panel

1
One waveform per video

The WAVEFORM panel shows a video's whole sound. Every pad cut from that video is a colored region across the top, so pads are just pieces of the same waveform. Tap a pad and the WAVEFORM panel shows the video it came from. Tap an empty pad to see a video that has no pads yet. Add videos with the camera button in the top bar.

2
Make a slice

Drag across the sound. It turns red with NEW on it and plays. Drag the red lines to fine tune it. SNAP keeps the length to whole sequencer steps, LOOP repeats it while you listen.

3
Put it on a pad

Hold the slice until it lifts, then drag. Drop it on any pad (a full pad gets replaced, Undo brings it back), or drop it on a bank block to put it in that bank's next empty pad. Rest on a bank block to flip to it first.

4
Edit a pad

Tap its region (or tap the pad) and drag its red lines, anywhere in the video, even past where it was first cut. It saves the moment you let go of a red line. Hold a region and drag it to another pad or bank to copy it there. Tapping a region plays it like hitting its pad, video included, and with REC on while the beat plays it records onto the step sequencer too.

5
Line up the picture

SLIP moves the picture a frame earlier or later while the sound stays put. On a new slice it is remembered for that video, so every slice after lines up too.

6
Zoom

Pinch, or double tap to zoom in on that spot (double tap again to see the whole video). − and + work too. The zoom stays put from pad to pad. Drag along the row of regions to slide.

Banks and pads

8 banks of 16

128 chops in all. Tap a bank block to put it on the pads. Hold a block until it lifts, then drag it to reorder. Your patterns follow the sounds.

Move a pad

Hold a pad for half a second, then drag it onto another pad. Drop it on a tiny square in the bank strip to send it to another bank, or rest on a bank block to flip to it. Then pick SWAP to trade places (the beat follows, so it sounds the same) or REPLACE to copy it over that pad (its steps play the new sound). Undo brings it back.

Clear

Hold the red button at the bottom of PAD BANKS and tap a bank block or a pad. Double tap the red button to keep clear mode on. Undo brings it back.

Play modes

ONE SHOT plays the whole chop. GATE stops when you let go, one step in the sequencer. LOOP repeats while held, and in the sequencer until that pad plays again.

Mute groups

Pads ring over each other. Put pads in the same MUTE GRP 1 to 4 to make them cut each other off, like an open and closed hat. To set a whole bank at once, hold its block in the bank strip and tap a MUTE GRP button, or change any PAD EDIT setting (PITCH, LEVEL, REVERSE, ONE SHOT, GATE or LOOP, FADE IN, FADE OUT, DECAY, FREEZE): all 16 pads take that setting. VIDEO SLIP stays per pad.

Make and trim pads

The TRIM strip at the bottom of the screen is always there. For pads cut from a loaded video it shows that video's whole sound, with every pad cut from it drawn as a colored region across the top. Regions can overlap: a pad that covers others gets its own row.

Make a pad: drag across the sound. The new region goes to the selected pad if it is empty, otherwise to the next empty pad in the bank (or the next bank with room). No pad tapping needed. Move it later with hold and drag in PAD BANKS.

Trim: tap a region to pick that pad and hear it. Drag the red lines to move its start and end anywhere in the video, even past where it was first cut. It saves the moment you let go of a red line. RESET puts it back, Undo too.

SNAP makes lengths whole sequencer steps. − and + zoom, and the zoom stays the same pad to pad. Drag along the row of regions to slide. Tap the TRIM bar to fold it to a thin bar (that saves too). If a pad's video is not loaded (old sessions saved without videos, demo pads), the strip shows just that pad and it can only be trimmed shorter.

Shape a pad

Pitch, level, reverse, fade in, fade out, decay, freeze frame and video slip. EDIT SLICE opens the WAVEFORM panel on that pad. Changing its start and end keeps its pitch, level and other settings.

Sequencer and patterns

Tracks

A to D. Pads play on the selected track. M mutes a track. A step shows bank:pad, like 2:4, when the sound is from another bank.

Longer patterns

Each track has its own length. Tap LNGTH to make the selected track 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars, hold it to set all 4 tracks at once. The pattern runs as long as its longest track; shorter tracks loop inside it and every track restarts together, so chains and exports still loop clean. When tracks differ, the button reads LNGTH B 2 and each track letter shows its bars. New bars start as copies. The numbers pick which bar you edit, FOLLOW makes the view follow the playhead.

Live record

Arm the red dot and play pads while it runs. Hits snap to the nearest step.

Shorter bars (skip steps)

Hold a page box (1, 2 and so on) until it reads 16 STEPS and every step on that page lights up. Tap steps to turn them off, and the count drops (13 STEPS). Tap the box again to finish. Turned off steps go gray and the playhead jumps over them on every track, so the bar gets shorter for odd time and stutters. A box with skips shows its count, like 2 · 13. Skips save with each pattern, copy with COPY TO NEXT, and export loops on the shorter length. Undo brings them back.

Erase a pad

While the beat plays, hold the red dot and hold a pad. Every hit of that pad the playhead passes gets erased, on all 4 tracks, and the pad lights red. Let go to stop. Hold more than one pad to erase them together. In KEYS, any key erases the selected pad. Undo brings it back.

Nudge

Hold a step until it gets a dashed outline, then push it early or late with ◀ and ▶. ALL nudges the whole track for a laid back or pushed feel.

Stacks

A step can hold up to 4 sounds. Pick another pad and tap the same step to add it. The step splits to show each one in its own color with its pad number: 2 sounds side by side, 3 with two on top and one below, 4 in quarters. Tap a step with one of its pads selected to take just that one out. Double tap a step to clear everything on it. Nudge moves the whole stack together.

KEYS and chords

The 16 pads become 16 notes of the selected pad, labeled with note names when it has a clear pitch. In KEYS, the chord row picks what one key plays: OFF single notes, MAJ, MIN, 7, m7, SUS, or KEY, which builds each chord from the key the scale reader found, so every chord fits the song. Tap a step to place the whole chord, and live record catches it too.

Patterns and chain

16 patterns. A new pattern starts when the current one ends. Turn on CHAIN and tap patterns in order to build a song. COPY TO NEXT duplicates one. Hold CLEAR TRACK or CLEAR PATTERN until the red bar fills to clear; let go early to cancel. Undo brings it back.

AUDIO FX

LOW · HIGHFilters on everything
1/2Slows the beat 25%, pitch stays
ECHOEcho in time with the beat
ST8 · ST16Stutter in 8th or 16th notes
REVHits play backwards
OCT↓An octave down, slowed like tape

Hold to punch in, double tap to lock it on, tap again to release. Most of them change the picture too: LOW blurs, HIGH inverts, ECHO leaves trails, REV tints, OCT↓ slows the video.

Track FX (2 per track)

GAINLevel, -12 to +12 dB
DRVDrive, light grit to heavy
LCUT · HCUTLow cut and high cut
LBST · HBSTLow boost and high boost, up to +12 dB
VERBReverb
DLYDelay in time with the beat

Hold a track slot to pick up to 2 and set each one's amount. Tap the slot to turn them on and off.

Video views and VIDEO FX

MIXEvery sound playing gets its own piece of the screen
FOCUSBig round eye: follows your selected track
1Only the latest hit
4One quarter per track
Track meters

Each track slot in AUDIO FX has a level light along its bottom in the track color. It moves with that track's sound after its FX, so you can see what is playing and how loud.

Track video

Each track has its own picture settings in VIDEO FX under TRACK VIDEO. Tap a track to switch its frame: FILL crops to fill (no black bars), FIT shows the whole frame, BLUR shows the whole frame over a blurred copy of itself. Hold a track for more: PLACE IN MIX (full screen, a half, a corner or a PIP box, so drums can fill the screen with vocals in a box), up to 2 FX (PUNCH, SHAKE, DRIFT, DUO, VHS), BLEND (SCREEN and MULTIPLY layer tracks over each other) and OPACITY. In FOCUS or 4-up view, drag the video to move a track's frame and pinch to zoom it; double tap resets. The FILL button above sets all 4 tracks at once.

Player shape

Tap the first button in VIDEO FX to pick the shape: 9:16 (TikTok, Reels), 4:5 (Instagram post), 1:1, 4:3, 16:9 (YouTube) or 1ST VID (matches your first video). It sets the export shape too. The player is always that shape, so there are no bars around it. Drag the handle under it and the whole picture grows or shrinks with it.

MIX layouts

STACK, GRID, PIP (new hits in corner windows), COLLAGE (hits land at random spots) and DIAGONAL.

Picture FX

ZOOM punches in on hits, SHK shakes, VHS adds scanlines and wobble, DUO tints each track its own color.

WAVE and TAG

WAVE draws the playing sound's waveform over the video. TAG puts your producer tag on every frame, type it and pick a corner.

Everything you see is in your exports. Zooming the player is only for you and doesn't change exports.

Save and export

Floppy disk

SAVE SESSION keeps every pad, pattern and setting in one .zip. INCLUDE VIDEOS: ON (the default) also keeps the full videos, so EDIT SLICE and VIDEO SLIP still work after you open it. Turn it off for a much smaller file. OPEN brings it back. SAVE SAMPLES gives you WAV files for any DAW. LOAD SAMPLES puts your own audio on pads.

INIT

In the floppy disk window: clear just the patterns, just the pads, or everything (which also resets all track FX and FX). Undo brings pads and patterns back.

Export

Pick the length (pattern, 1 to 8 bars or the whole chain), how many times it loops (1× to 16×) and the shape (9:16, 1:1, 16:9 or your first video), then RECORD. It starts on the downbeat and ends right on the bar.

In the home screen app, tap the save button when the file is ready, then pick Save to Files or Save Video. Work also autosaves on your phone. Full original videos aren't saved, so add a video again to re-chop it.

Tips for better beats

Shoot at 30 fps

Steady light, phone still, the sound close to the mic. Clean hits chop better.

Chords that fit

Chop one clean note, turn on KEYS and pick KEY in the chord row. Every key plays a chord that belongs to your video's key.

Match keys

Check the key on each video's tab before mixing samples, so your chops sit together.

Hats in a mute group

Put the open and closed hat in the same group so the closed one chokes the open one.

Fatten the low end

LBST on the 808 track and LCUT on the sample track keeps the bass clear.

Fix late video once

Phone cameras often show the picture a few frames late. Set SLIP in the WAVEFORM panel on a new slice and every chop after it from that video comes out lined up.

Brand every export

Turn on TAG and every video you post carries your name.

Fixes

A pad shows only itself in the WAVEFORM panel

Sessions saved before INCLUDE VIDEOS, or with it off, keep every chop but not the full videos. Add the same video again with the camera button in the top bar. Videos you load are kept in this browser with your pads, so closing and reopening the app keeps every pad tied to its video. Loading a different session or starting a new one replaces them. That pad and every pad cut from the same video link back, so EDIT SLICE and VIDEO SLIP work again.

Export picture freezes partway

The picture stops if the screen dims or locks while recording, even though the sound keeps going. VBM-16 now asks the phone to stay awake during exports. If it still happens on long exports, set Settings, Display and Brightness, Auto-Lock to a longer time, and keep VBM-16 on screen until the clip appears.

No sound after recording with the camera

Recording turns on the iPhone mic, which can interrupt VBM-16's sound. If a clip comes in silent, tap TRY AGAIN. Still silent: make sure the ring switch isn't on silent, or record in the Camera app and add the clip with the camera icon.

"Listening to the audio" takes a while

On iPhone, VBM-16 plays the clip silently once to grab the sound, about as long as the clip, up to the first 60 seconds.

The finger lands late on the hit

Use SLIP in the WAVEFORM panel (or VIDEO SLIP in PAD EDIT) one frame at a time until it lines up.

"All 8 banks are full"

Auto chop never overwrites. Clear a bank with the red button in PAD BANKS, then add the video again.

A click at the start or end of a chop

Raise FADE IN or FADE OUT on that pad a few ms.

The key reads wrong

Busy drums and noisy clips are hard to read. Trust your ears and use the second choice it shows when it isn't sure.

The update isn't showing

Close the home screen app fully and open it again while you have signal.
