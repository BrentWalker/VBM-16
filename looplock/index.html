HOW IT WORKS

Video and audio scrubbersThe strip under the video and the strip on top of the waveform show the whole clip. They move together. Drag either one to jump anywhere.
WaveformDrag the red S (start) and blue L (loop point) flags to set your loop. Pinch to zoom. Double tap to zoom all the way out, double tap again to go back.
Nudge rowRed arrows move the start, blue arrows move the loop point. The middle button sets how far each tap moves: 1 frame, 10 ms, 1 ms or 0.1 ms. Hold an arrow to keep moving.
PlayPlays your loop around and around with no gap at all. This is the perfect loop.
Bar skipPlays the first section of the loop and then the last one, over and over, so you hear the seam fast. Hold it to choose 2, 4, 8 or 16 sections.
LockFreezes the loop length. Then ÷2 and ×2 halve or double it, the arrows slide the whole loop, and you can press, hold and drag inside the loop to slide it.
Start, EndJump to the start or the end of the loop.
XFCrossfade while you listen: blends the end of the loop into the start so it doesn't click. Tap to change.
MetronomeClicks on the section lines. Tap for on, again for double time, again for off.
Export buttonsTap one to export and open the share sheet, then pick the app. IG, YT and TT come out vertical 9:16, the first button keeps the video's shape. Hold any of them to set its own offset and crossfade.
New videoThe camera icon in the corner of the video. Pick from your library or Files.

WHY SOCIAL LOOPS AREN'T PERFECT

Inside Loop Lock your loop never stops, so it's perfect. Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and Photos use their own video players, and most of them pause for a split second (about 75 to 200 ms) every time a video starts over. The sound goes silent and the picture freezes for that moment.
No video file can remove that pause, because it happens inside the app's player. What a file can do is keep your beat on time across it. That's what the offset is for.
Each app pauses for a different amount, and it can change with app or iPhone updates. That's why every export button keeps its own numbers.

HOW TO DIAL IN AN EXPORT BUTTON

Fastest wayUse Calibrate at the top of this page. It measures the offset for you. Then shape the pause with release and attack below.
1. Offset firstThe offset shortens the end of the file by the length of the app's pause, so the next downbeat lands on time. Hold the button, change the offset, export, post it privately and listen to it loop.
2. Listen to passes 2, 3 and 4Apps often treat the first pass differently. If the beat comes back late, raise the offset. If it comes back early, lower it. Go in 10 ms steps, then 2 or 3 ms steps once it's close.
3. Then shape the pauseRelease fades the end of the loop down into the app's pause, so it sounds like a quick dip instead of a cut. Try 20 to 60 ms. Attack fades back in after the pause: keep it at 1 to 5 ms so your downbeat still hits hard. Both only work when the offset is above 0.
CrossfadeBlends the end of your loop into its start. It matters most for apps with no pause (offset 0, like TikTok). Keep it at 2 to 5 ms unless you hear a click.
4. Change one number at a timeThen the button remembers it until you change it again.

TIPS FOR BETTER LOOPS

Frame perfect temposAt 30 fps, one bar lands on a whole number of video frames at 72, 75, 80, 90, 96, 100, 120, 144, 150, 160 and 180 bpm. At those tempos nothing gets stretched.
Give the end a breathThe app's pause lands at the very end of your loop. If the last moment is already sparse (hats out, 808 tail fading), the pause sounds like part of the groove.
Match the pictureThe picture freezes on the last frame during the pause. If your hands are in about the same spot at the start and end of the loop, nobody sees it.
Shoot at 30 fps60 and 24 fps clips get converted by the apps, which adds its own little timing errors.
Upload settingsInstagram: post as a Reel and turn on Upload at highest quality. TikTok: turn on Allow high quality uploads. Don't add the app's own music on top.

Loop Lock Master cleans the low end, adds phone speaker presence, glues and warms the beat, then sets it to the loudness you pick (LUFS) with a limiter that never clips (-1 dB true peak). It works around the loop as a circle, so the seam doesn't pump. Platforms turn loud uploads down to about -14, so louder isn't always better: -14 to -12 is the sweet spot.

Offset shortens the file by the app's restart pause so the beat lands on time. Release fades the end into that pause, attack fades back in after it (both only used when offset is above 0). Crossfade blends the loop's end into its start. All in ms. Seam Quietest starts and ends the file at the loop's quietest moment, so the app's restart silence lands where the beat is already quiet.
