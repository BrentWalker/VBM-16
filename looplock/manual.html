Put it on your iPhone

1
Open the app in Safari

brentwalker.github.io/VBM-16/looplock

2
Add to Home Screen

Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

3
Open it from the icon

It runs full screen, works without signal after the first open, and reopens where you left off.

Make your first loop

1
Import

Tap Import video and pick a clip from Photos or Files. Loop Lock finds the tempo and suggests a loop.

2
Find the loop

Drag the red S flag to where the loop starts and the blue L flag to where it loops back. Pinch to zoom.

3
Play it

Tap ▶. It loops with no gap at all. Listen to the seam.

4
Nudge

Red arrows move the start, blue arrows move the loop point. The middle button sets the step: 1 frame, 10 ms, 1 ms or 0.1 ms. Hold an arrow to keep moving. Keep going until the seam disappears.

5
Check it with Bar Skip

Tap BAR SKIP. It plays the first section, then the last, over and over, so you hear the seam fast. Hold it to pick 2, 4, 8 or 16 sections.

6
Lock it

Tap LOCK. The length is frozen. Now ÷2 and ×2 halve or double it, the arrows slide the whole loop, and you can press, hold and drag inside the loop to slide it.

7
Export

Tap the button for where it's going. Pick the app in the share sheet.

Video scrubber and audio scrubber

The strip under the video and the strip on top of the waveform show the whole clip and move together. Drag either to jump anywhere.

Waveform

Your loop in high resolution. Double tap to zoom all the way out, double tap again to go back.

Nudge row

Unlocked: nudge the start and loop point. Locked: ÷2, slide, step, slide, ×2.

Lock · Stop · Play · Metronome · Bar Skip

Stop ■ stops all playback and cancels an export. The metronome clicks on the section lines: tap for on, again for double time, again for off.

START · XF · END

Jump to the loop's start or end. XF is the crossfade you hear while playing. Tap to change it.

Reading the label: 178·4·Q·M means offset 178, crossfade 4, Seam Quietest, Master on.

EQ tab

Three knobs

LOW is a shelf at 150 Hz, MID is a wide bell at 2.5 kHz, HIGH is a shelf at 8 kHz. Drag a knob up to boost or down to cut: LOW and HIGH go 18 dB either way, MID goes 12. Double tap a knob to reset it to 0.

You hear the EQ while you play, and it's baked into every export, before Loop Lock Master. A new video starts flat.

Save tab

Save Project

Keeps the video, the loop, the lock, divisions, metronome and EQ exactly as they are.

Projects

Lists your saved projects. Open one to pick up right where you saved it. Delete asks once more before it removes anything. Projects live on this phone only.

Button settings (hold any export button)

Offset

Shortens the file by the app's restart pause, so the beat comes back on time. The most important setting.

Seam: Quietest or Loop start

Quietest starts and ends the file at the quietest spot in your loop, so the app's pause and Instagram's start mute land where the beat is already quiet. Loop start begins the file on your downbeat.

Master

Loop Lock Master: cleans the low end, adds phone speaker presence, glues the beat, adds warmth, then sets the loudness you pick with peaks held at -1 dB. It works around the loop as a circle, so the seam doesn't pump. Choose No to export the mix untouched.

Loudness

How loud the master comes out, in LUFS. Apps turn loud uploads down to about -14, so -14 to -12 is the sweet spot.

Crossfade, attack, release

Small pop fixers. Crossfade blends the loop's end into its start. Release fades the end into the pause, attack fades back in. With Seam Quietest they barely matter.

Every button keeps its own numbers until you change them.

Calibrate a button

1
Pick the app and make the test clip

Gear, then pick IG, YT, TT or Photos, then Make test clip. The share sheet opens.

2
Post it and record it

Post it privately (or save it to Photos), play it, and screen record at least 4 loops. Microphone off.

3
Import the recording

Tap Import the recording. Loop Lock measures the pause to the millisecond and saves it to that button's offset.

Why social loops aren't perfect

Inside Loop Lock the loop never stops. Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and Photos use their own players, and most of them stop, jump back to the start and begin again every time. That restart leaves a split second of silence, about 150 to 200 ms. Instagram also mutes about the first 45 ms of the file on every restart.

No file can remove that pause, because it happens inside the app's player. A file can keep your beat on time across it (offset) and put the cut in a quiet spot (Seam Quietest). That's what Loop Lock does.

Tips for better loops

Frame perfect tempos

At 30 fps, one bar lands on a whole number of frames at 72, 75, 80, 90, 96, 100, 120, 144, 150, 160 and 180 bpm.

Give the end a breath

A sparse last moment (hats out, 808 tail fading) makes the app's pause sound like part of the groove.

Match the picture

The picture freezes on the last frame during the pause. Hands in about the same spot at the start and end hide it.

Shoot at 30 fps

60 and 24 fps clips get converted by the apps, which adds timing errors.

Upload settings

Instagram: post as a Reel with Upload at highest quality on. TikTok: turn on Allow high quality uploads. Don't add the app's own music on top.

Fixes

"Could not pull the audio straight from this file"

Tap Read audio by playing. If that fails, export the video from Photos as Most Compatible, or screen record it.

The beat comes back late on a platform

Raise that button's offset in 5 to 10 ms steps. Early: lower it.

A click at the seam

Use Seam Quietest and export a fresh file. Still there: raise crossfade to 10.

The update isn't showing

Close the home screen app fully and open it again.
